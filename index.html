The cognitive tasks I built and redesigned

Each is a short game students play, built in Unreal Engine 5 and C++. Below the screenshots are the eight skills they measure.

Veyra's twelve game tasks

Select a skill to see its task.

  • Task 03

    Working memory

    What the pupil does
    Sees a sequence of five coloured shapes, holds it in mind and gives it back in reverse order.
    What it measures
    Holding information in mind while using it. Giving the sequence back in reverse means working with what is remembered, not only repeating it.
    Tasks 05 and 11

    Verbal memory

    What the pupil does
    Taps a sound button to listen, then chooses from the five pictures below it. The delayed task returns to it later on.
    What it measures
    Remembering and recalling words, both straight away and again after a delay.
    Task 07

    Processing speed

    What the pupil does
    Sees a target shape to look for, above three shapes in a row, and picks out the one that matches.
    What it measures
    How quickly simple information is taken in and acted on.
    Task 09

    Inhibition

    What the pupil does
    Follows the instruction “Look for the Opposite” and chooses from four shapes around a centre point.
    What it measures
    Stopping an automatic response when it is the wrong one, here the pull to pick the shape that matches.
    Task 10

    Task shifting

    What the pupil does
    Follows the instruction “Look for the Same” with four shapes around a centre point, while the rule changes.
    What it measures
    Switching between rules or tasks, and keeping up when the rule changes.
    Task 08

    Selective attention

    What the pupil does
    Looks for a target shape among four shapes around a centre point.
    What it measures
    Focusing on what matters and ignoring distractions.
    Tasks 06 and 12

    Visuospatial memory

    What the pupil does
    Remembers a board of five coloured shapes, with a 30-second timer in the immediate task. In the delayed task the pupil rebuilds the board later from a panel.
    What it measures
    Remembering where things are and how they are arranged, both straight away and again after a delay.
    Task 01

    Planning

    What the pupil does
    Puts five coloured shapes in order to match a target row.
    What it measures
    Thinking ahead and ordering steps towards a goal.

Project Extract

A single-player tactical extraction shooter in Unreal Engine 5.7. The player infiltrates a compound, rescues a captive and fights out to extraction alongside an AI companion they command.

The game

You go into a compound, find a captive and get them out alive, with an AI companion at your side. You command the companion through three behaviour modes and a set of orders, like sending them to search a room.

The enemy force is eight archetypes running on one shared behaviour tree. They are coordinated into squads, and a director system paces the fight by controlling when reinforcements arrive.

The game

You play a wizard fighting through more than a dozen dungeons, including side dungeons, on the way to a boss. You click to move or use WASD, and cast from four spell slots and two passive slots.

Kills give XP. Each level brings attribute points for your four primary stats and spell points to unlock, equip and level up spells. Checkpoints save your progress to one of three save slots.

How it works

From key press to damage

How a spell lands, built on Unreal's Gameplay Ability System.

One spell, start to finish
  1. 01InputA key or click sends its input tag: a mouse button or keys 1 to 4.
  2. 02AbilityThe spell on that tag activates, pays its mana cost and starts its cooldown.
  3. 03Gameplay effectThe spell hits and applies a damage effect, carrying the damage type and amount.
  4. 04Damage calculationReads the attacker's and the target's stats, in the five steps below.
  5. 05Attribute changeHealth drops, and any debuff and knockback land. A kill sends XP.
  6. 06Floating textThe number shows over the target: normal, blocked or critical.
The damage calculation

Five steps, using stats from both sides

Attacker's statTarget's stat
  1. 1
    Debuff rollThe spell's debuff chance, lowered by the target's resistance to that element. A success sets the debuff's damage, duration and frequency.
    Resistance to the element
  2. 2
    BlockRolled against the target's Block Chance. A block halves the damage.
    Block Chance
  3. 3
    ArmorArmor Penetration lowers the target's Armor, then the Armor left lowers the damage, both weighted by level.
    Armor PenetrationArmor
  4. 4
    Critical hitThe chance is Critical Hit Chance less the target's Critical Hit Resistance. A crit doubles the damage and adds Critical Hit Damage.
    Critical Hit ChanceCritical Hit DamageCritical Hit Resistance
  5. 5
    ResultThe final number goes to the target's Incoming Damage, ready to lower its Health.
